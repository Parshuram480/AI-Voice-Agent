2. Property Booking, Token Amount & Refund Terms
- Token Deposit (EOI): A minimum 2% token deposit reserves any available residential property unit for 7 business days.
- Token Refund Policy: 100% full refund of the token deposit if the buyer cancels within the 7-day reservation window prior to signing the Agreement to Sell.
- Earnest Money Deposit (EMD): 10% of the total property agreement value is due upon formal agreement signing.

3. Property Site Visits & Inspection Guidelines
- Free Guided Site Visits: Available Monday through Saturday with 24 hours advance scheduling.
- VIP Pick-Up Facility: Complimentary pick-up and drop service available for qualified buyers within a 30-mile radius.

4. Brokerage Charges & Home Loan Partner Guidelines
- Standard Brokerage Fee: 2% of total transaction value for secondary resale properties (0% brokerage on direct developer partner launches).
- Home Loan Assistance: Pre-approved home loan processing with partner financial institutions starting at interest rates from 8.25% p.a.

5. Escalation & Senior Advisor Transfer Rules
- Senior Specialist Transfer: If a client requests custom developer payment plans, bulk multi-unit investor discounts (>3 units), or legal title deed verification, transfer the call immediately to a Senior Real Estate Specialist.
