HEALTHCARE HOSPITAL & PATIENT CARE GUIDELINES

1. Appointment Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
Patients can cancel or reschedule appointments up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled time without any fee. Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance or no-shows will incur a $25 cancellation fee.

2. Operating Hours & Emergency Services
The Emergency Department (ED) and Urgent Care Unit operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Outpatient Specialist Consultation Clinics operate Monday through Saturday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.

3. Health Insurance & Billing Policy
We accept all major health insurance providers including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, Medicare, and UnitedHealthcare. Co-payments, deductibles, and non-covered services must be settled at the time of check-in.

4. Prescription Refill Guidelines
Prescription refill requests require 48 hours for processing by the primary care physician. Controlled substances require an in-person physical consultation before a new prescription can be renewed.

5. Hospital Visiting Hours & ICU Rules
General inpatient ward visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. A maximum of 2 visitors are allowed at any given time per patient in ICU and Intensive Care units to ensure a quiet recovery environment.
